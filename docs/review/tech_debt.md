## Debt profile summary

The debt is **concentrated in the engineering system, not the domain design**: the four Critical items (CI, observability, tests, and their interaction) are all "infrastructure around the code" and are each individually cheap relative to their risk. The domain-level debts (money-as-double, invoice reassignment, stock-clamp divergence) share one root cause — *decisions made for offline convenience that quietly break global invariants* — and all three must land before the fraud/BIR milestones give those invariants legal weight.

Recommended reading order for new contributors: `executive_summary.md` → this file → `roadmap.md` for sequencing.
